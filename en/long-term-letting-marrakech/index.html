Letting your property long term in Marrakech

Long-term letting is the simplest option for an owner who wants regular income without any involvement. In Marrakech, demand for furnished and unfurnished year-round rentals is strong: executives, expatriates, students, Moroccan families, European retirees.

Dar Serenity manages apartments, villas and houses in Guéliz, Hivernage, Agdal, Targa, Casablanca Road and the Palmeraie. We set the right rent, select the tenant and follow the lease through to its end.

What our management includes

  • Rent estimate based on actual lets in the area
  • Listing, viewings, tenant selection and checks
  • Watertight lease, photo inventory, secured deposit
  • Rent collected and transferred on the 5th of every month
  • Receipts, reminders, arrears follow-up
  • Maintenance and works on quotation, with your approval
  • Annual summary for your accountant

What letting management costs in Marrakech

Our fee is simple: one month's rent ex. VAT at move-in, and nothing else. Maintenance work is done on quotation, with your approval. No set-up fee, no minimum term.

You receive your rent by transfer on the 5th of every month, with a statement, and an annual summary for your accountant.

How it works

1. Visit and rent estimate. We visit the property and propose a realistic rent based on actual lets in the area.

2. Mandate and letting. Photos, listing, viewings, tenant selection with income and guarantor checks.

3. Lease and inventory. Lease compliant with Moroccan law, photo inventory, secured deposit.

4. Lease follow-up. Collection, receipts, reminders, arrears, maintenance, renewal or tenant departure.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does letting management cost with Dar Serenity?

One month's rent ex. VAT at move-in. No monthly fee, no set-up fee. Works are invoiced on quotation, with your approval.

How do you select tenants?

ID, proof of income, guarantor where needed, references. We only present complete files.

What happens if the tenant does not pay?

Immediate reminder, formal notice, then proceedings with our partner lawyer if needed. You are informed at every step.

Do you manage furnished and unfurnished properties?

Yes, both. We advise you on the best option for the area and demand.

Can I switch from long-term to short-term letting?

Yes, at the end of the lease. You can change plan at no cost.
