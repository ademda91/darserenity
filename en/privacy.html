Privacy policy

Dar Serenity Properties takes the protection of your personal data seriously, in accordance with Moroccan law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data and, for residents of the European Union, the GDPR.

Data we collect

When you use the contact form or write to us on WhatsApp, we collect your name, your phone number or e-mail address, and the information you give about your property or your project. The form does not store anything on our servers: it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message that you send yourself.

Purpose

To reply to your request, prepare an estimate or a selection of properties, and, if we work together, perform the mandate or the contract. We do not sell or rent your data.

Recipients and transfers outside the EU

Your messages go through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd). The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When a transaction takes place, the data required is shared with the notary and, where applicable, the lawyer involved. Dar Serenity Properties is based in Morocco: the transfer is necessary for the performance of a contract or of pre-contractual measures taken at your request (GDPR art. 49.1.b).

Retention

Requests that do not lead to a contract are deleted after 12 months. Contractual and accounting documents are kept for 10 years, as required by law.

Cookies and measurement

Only after you accept the banner shown on your first visit, this site may use audience and advertising measurement tools: Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads (Google Ireland Ltd) and the Meta pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd). They measure visits and the performance of our advertising. If you refuse, nothing is loaded. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies; the banner will appear again. Technical local storage (language, consent choice) does not require consent.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, object to, delete, restrict and port your data. We answer within one month. You may also contact the CNDP (Morocco) or the authority of your country of residence (for example the CNIL in France). To exercise your rights, write to contact@darserenity.com.
